Use this to sync with a partner and build a new partnership. Generally takes 2 to 8 practices to get through everything. Start with dancing, alternate 15 - 30 minute intervals of the progression and 5 to 10 minutes of dancing. End practice with dancing.

Part 1
1. [[balboa basic - up]] in [[The Six Directions of Movement]]. The sideways one is tricky but very important to avoid true auto pilot.
2. 1/2 basic and out and in. Do the out and in on the right and the left and in the The Six Directions of Movement|six directions
3. out and in six directions

Part 2
1. come around, check lilt for powering the rotational motion
2. starter step to the left
3. compare contrast rotating up basic with come around

Part 3
1. Down basic 6 directions
2. crab walks
3. paddles

Part 4
1. toss out come around - this one often takes *forever* because it is often focused. Relax, take time to establish what you both want and be able to lead and follow it.
2. grape vine to turn

Part 5
1. reverse toss out
2. swivels
3. multi turns

Part 6
1. send out 5 versions
2. follower lolly variations
3. swivels as up / down basic

Part 7
- anything that feels funky that comes up in dancing
- i.e. freezes, cross overs, grape vines, pop turn from lollies, elbow catches

Part 8
- develop move set as a partnership
