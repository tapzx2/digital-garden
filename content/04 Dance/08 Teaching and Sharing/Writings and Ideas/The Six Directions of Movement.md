The six directions of movement in dance are
- forward
- backward
- right
- left
- rotate right
- rotate left

Think about dance moves and how you can apply all six directions to develop deep levels of proficiency and skill. Not all will apply, but many of the basics will.

Can you do, in all six directions:
- out and ins from balboa?
- A salsa basic?
- A sugar push?
