```
S     Q S     Q
1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8
L L L R L L L R
```

Can be flipped to start on R of course. Consider putting a [[shag rhythm - triple]] to switch sides and make bilateral.

Can be made bilateral with a little effort i.e.

```
S     Q S   Q Q
1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8
L L L R L L R L
```

or

```
S     Q S      
1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8
L L L R L L L L
```
