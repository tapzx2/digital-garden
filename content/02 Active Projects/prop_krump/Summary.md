With [[SB1415P Arizona State ADU Law]] (Jan 2025) and [[House Bill 2721]] (Jan 2026) providing less friction for missing middle housing, @Lucas Krump is investigating the development of properties. With new laws, there is now little restriction on adding single ADU. Two story fourplexes are now more possible.

[[1101 West Portland]] is the largest lot, but the least opportunity. In an affluent neighborhood, expect significant pushback. Property already has a detached unit. Probably wouldn't pencil to add more at this time.

[[1637 East Coronado Road]] can easily accommodate a second building. Has a lot density precedent in the neighborhood. More ambitious ideas include adding a triplex in the back or purchasing the neighboring lot, demoing, and making a large luxury fourplex with possible back half second story.

[[2506 North Dayton]] is already a triplex. Dubious if adding more density to the property would be worth the money. Unfortunate, because neighborhood already has a 2nd story precedent. Parking minimums already met with spaces. Back half second story unit a possibility, but probably not worth the money. Left neighbor recently purchased.
