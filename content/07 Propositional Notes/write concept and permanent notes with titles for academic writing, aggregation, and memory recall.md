## Aggregation Titles
MOC style titles. They are vague and in the note, either a list MOC style, or the evidence from various places needed to support the title.

Examples from Collins2022Zettelkasten101
 "Address yourself tomorrow"
 "Adhere to Aristotle's Three Unities"
"Admirable or fun creators"
"Advantages of web 3 over web 2"
"AI NFT art tools"
"Aim for the 4th part of the rich dad's quadrate"
"All creative people have power now"
"Allow a room for error"
"Always end emails with a cliff hanger"
## Propositional Titles
Powerful. source: Matuschak0000WritingNoteTitles

By distilling down a note into a single claim, it will help you focus the writing and provide islands aka Use an archipelago of ideas to write quickly and well for you to build between with writing. Retrieving a _claim_ from memory much faster than a vague title.

Examples from Seitz2021StarterPack

- "love thy neighbor" to
- "Loving your neighbor is vital for a well-functioning society". 

- "Relationship between art and truth" to
- "Art is a means of disclosing reliable, true knowledge"

- Literacy of Puritans in late 18th century America" to
- "American Puritans of the late 18th century were highly literate"

- "Learning through linking" to
- "Linking ideas together helps us remember ideas and use them"
