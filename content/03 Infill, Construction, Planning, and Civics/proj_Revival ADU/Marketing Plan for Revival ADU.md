### Brokers and Agents
Focus on high-touch, high-value clients like real estate agents and brokers specializing in multi-family properties. Use a personalized, relationship-driven approach:

1. Find them: Outsource data collection to Upwork or Fiverr. See: [[Data Collection on Real Estate Agents and Brokers in the LA Basin Job Brief]].
2. Reach out: Use personal calls or emails. ChatGPT can assist with initial scripts. [[ChatGPT Outreach Messages for Revival ADU]]. Refine your approach after a few attempts, then delegate outreach if needed.
3. Build Trust: Deliver value through high-quality conversations. Bring actionable insights to meetings i.e. [[Bring ADU Numbers for the Property to the Meeting]].
4. Maintain Relationships: Follow up regularly. Use a system to schedule reconnections or draw on ChatGPT’s ideas for engagement. [[ChatGPT Ideas for Developing Touches for High Value Contacts]].
